bone tag is a game meant to be played daily, where you are presented with 5 bones, one after the after, and you have to select where you are. You get points for how close you are. The first three are easy and get 1x multiplier, and the last two are harder, more obscure bones, and get 3x multiplier. You are presented with a 3D model of a skeleton that you can rotate and zoom around in order to find the bones. You select by holding to tap. The whole game is inspired by maptag (see https://maptap.gg/) but for bones.
